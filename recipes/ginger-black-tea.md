# Ginger Black Tea

A clear, warming Assam tea with a pronounced fresh-ginger flavour. The ginger is simmered before the tea is added, giving each ingredient an appropriate extraction time and avoiding excessive tannin.

## Ingredients

- 450 ml water
- 15 g fresh ginger, thinly sliced and lightly crushed
- 8 g Assam black tea
- 20 g brown sugar, or to taste

## Equipment

- Small lidded saucepan
- Stirring spoon
- Fine tea strainer
- Heatproof serving pot or two cups
- Kitchen scale and measuring cup

## Method

1. Combine the water and fresh ginger in the saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat. Partially cover the pan and simmer for 6-8 minutes.
3. Turn off the heat and add the Assam tea.
4. Cover and steep for 3-4 minutes. For fine CTC tea, steep for only 2-3 minutes.
5. Strain immediately into a heatproof pot or cups.
6. Stir in the brown sugar and taste. Add more only if needed.
7. Serve hot.

## Adjustments

- For more ginger heat, lightly crush or grate part of the fresh ginger instead of extending the tea steep.
- For a softer cup, reduce the tea to 7 g or steep for 3 minutes.

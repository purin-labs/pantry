# Black Sesame Milk - Cafe Production Recipe

Prepare one refrigerated black-sesame concentrate each morning, then combine it with milk to order. Grinding, slurry cooking, and sesame-base production happen once per batch.

## Formula

### Ten-drink concentrate batch

- 220 g roasted black sesame seeds
- 120 g brown cane sugar
- 20 g glutinous rice flour
- 400 g water, plus hot water for final weight adjustment if needed
- 2 g fine salt

**Target finished concentrate:** 760 g, providing ten 76 g doses.

### Per drink

- 76 g refrigerated black-sesame concentrate
- 210 g cold whole milk
- Black sesame garnish, optional and restrained

**Approximate drink weight before garnish:** 286 g.

## Recommended equipment

- Kitchen scale accurate to at least 1 g
- Sheet tray, if refreshing the sesame roast
- Commercial spice grinder
- High-speed blender rated for the mixture's operating temperature
- Saucepan, whisk, and heatproof spatula
- Probe thermometer
- Shallow stainless-steel container or small food-safe bags for rapid cooling
- Ice bath
- Two sanitized 380 g bottles or covered wide-mouth containers
- Dedicated heated milk mixer or induction milk frother with removable jug
- Fine chinois or metal sieve, optional

## Morning production SOP

### 1. Assess or refresh the sesame roast

Because the seeds are already roasted, first test the recipe without reheating them. Skip this step when their aroma is strong enough.

If the sesame needs refreshing:

1. Spread the seeds in a thin layer on a tray.
2. Heat at 130-140°C for 4-6 minutes.
3. Stir once halfway through.
4. Remove the seeds as soon as their fragrance intensifies. Do not allow smoke, sharp bitterness, or a burnt smell.
5. Cool until warm.

An oven provides more repeatable results than a frying pan.

### 2. Grind the sesame

1. Divide the sesame into portions of approximately 50-75 g.
2. Pulse each portion in a dedicated spice grinder until it becomes a fine meal.
3. Continue until the meal begins clumping and releasing oil.
4. Transfer each portion to the high-speed blender.

Perfect smoothness is unnecessary at this stage. The hot mixture and final blending step complete the refinement. Grind the sesame fresh each morning to protect its aroma.

### 3. Cook the rice-flour mixture

1. Add 400 g cold water to a saucepan.
2. Whisk in 20 g glutinous rice flour until completely smooth.
3. Heat while stirring continuously.
4. Bring the mixture to 85-90°C.
5. When it becomes slightly glossy and thickened, continue stirring for about 60 seconds.
6. Remove the pan from the heat.
7. Stir in 120 g brown cane sugar and 2 g salt until completely dissolved.

Incorporate the cooked mixture into the sesame base immediately. Do not store a separate raw or cooked slurry.

### 4. Blend and standardize the concentrate

1. While the cooked mixture is still hot, add it to the blender containing the ground sesame.
2. Blend on high for 90-120 seconds.
3. Stop and scrape down the sides if necessary.
4. Blend again until the concentrate is glossy and uniform.
5. If coarse particles remain, pass the concentrate through a fine chinois or metal sieve.
6. Weigh the finished batch. If it weighs less than 760 g, blend in enough hot water to reach exactly 760 g.

The listed ingredients weigh 762 g before cooking. Normal evaporation should bring the mixture below the 760 g target; add water only when the finished batch is underweight.

> **Hot-blender safety:** Do not tightly seal hot liquid in a domestic blender unless its manufacturer explicitly permits it. Use a vented lid or cool the mixture to the blender's permitted operating temperature.

### 5. Rapidly cool and bottle

1. Transfer the concentrate immediately to either a shallow stainless-steel container over an ice bath or small sealed bags laid flat in an ice bath.
2. Cool it rapidly.
3. Transfer the cold concentrate into two sanitized 380 g bottles, each holding five drink portions.
4. Label each bottle with its production and discard times.
5. Refrigerate at 5°C or below.

## Per-order service - preferred method

Use a dedicated heated milk mixer or induction milk frother with a removable jug.

1. Shake or stir the refrigerated concentrate until uniform.
2. Weigh 76 g concentrate into the jug.
3. Add 210 g cold whole milk.
4. Heat and texture the drink to 62-64°C.
5. Pour into a preheated cup.
6. Add a restrained sesame garnish, if desired.

**Expected service time:** approximately 45-60 seconds.

## Alternative service methods

### Espresso machine with a clean steam wand

This method keeps sesame out of the steam wand.

1. Add 76 g concentrate to the serving cup.
2. Warm the concentrate to 45-50°C in a commercial microwave or on a small induction station.
3. Steam 210 g milk separately to 63-65°C.
4. Add the first 30-40 g steamed milk to the concentrate.
5. Whisk vigorously to form a smooth emulsion.
6. Pour in the remaining microfoamed milk.

Determine microwave timing with the specific appliance and verify the result using a probe thermometer.

### Fast steam-wand method

Combine 76 g concentrate and 210 g milk in a dedicated pitcher, then steam them together. This provides fast heating and strong emulsification, though it turns the steam wand into a sesame-allergen contact surface.

Required controls:

- Use a clearly marked, sesame-only pitcher.
- Wipe and purge the steam wand immediately after service.
- Maintain a documented cleaning procedure.
- Train staff to communicate that other drinks may be exposed to sesame cross-contact.

## Storage and food-safety controls

- Keep the concentrate at 5°C or below.
- Use it on the day it is made.
- During initial production trials, use 24 hours as the absolute limit.
- Discard the remaining concentrate at closing.
- Do not determine a multi-day shelf life from smell or appearance. Any extension requires the business's HACCP process and should be supported by microbiological shelf-life testing.
- Do not leave cooked rice-based concentrate at room temperature for two hours or longer.
- Do not hot-hold the full batch; extended heat continues thickening the starch and degrades consistency and aroma.
- During a rush, time-label only the amount forecast for the next hour. Follow the business's HACCP discard rule, and do not return warmed concentrate to refrigeration.

## Allergen control

Sesame is an allergen and the recipe's default formulation also contains milk. Use dedicated equipment where specified, document cleaning procedures, and communicate cross-contact accurately to customers and staff.

## Batch quality-control record

Record the following for every batch:

- Ingredient weights
- Final batch weight
- Production time
- Cooling completion time
- Refrigeration temperature
- Discard time
- Number of servings produced

## Launch configuration

- Make one fresh concentrate batch each morning.
- Use a high-speed blender and dedicated spice grinder.
- Store the batch in two refrigerated 380 g bottles.
- Dose the concentrate by weight at 76 g per drink.
- Use a dedicated induction milk heater or frother.
- Discard any remaining concentrate at closing.

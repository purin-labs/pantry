# Ginger Pot-Boiled Milk Tea

A tea-forward Assam milk tea with fresh ginger, moderate sweetness, and a smooth milk finish. Ginger and tea are extracted separately, then the strained tea is finished with milk at a controlled temperature.

## Ingredients

- 400 ml water
- 180 ml whole milk
- 15 g fresh ginger, thinly sliced and lightly crushed
- 9 g Assam black tea
- 25 g brown sugar, or to taste
- 0.25 g ground dried ginger, optional

## Equipment

- Small lidded saucepan
- Stirring spoon
- Fine tea strainer
- Probe thermometer, recommended
- Heatproof serving pot or two cups
- Kitchen scale and measuring cup

## Method

1. Combine the water and fresh ginger in the saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat. Partially cover the pan and simmer for 6-8 minutes.
3. Add the Assam tea and maintain a very gentle simmer for 3 minutes.
4. Strain the ginger tea into a clean saucepan, removing the tea leaves and spices.
5. Add the milk, brown sugar, and optional ground ginger.
6. Heat gently while stirring until the drink reaches 75-80°C. Do not let it simmer or boil.
7. Taste and adjust the sweetness if needed.
8. Pour into preheated cups and serve hot.

## Adjustments

- For a creamier drink, increase the milk to 200 ml. For a more tea-forward drink, reduce it to 150 ml.
- For stronger ginger, lightly crush or grate part of the fresh ginger. Avoid extending the tea extraction, which increases astringency more than ginger flavour.
- For a more traditional, robust pot-boiled character, add the milk without straining, simmer everything together for 1 minute, and strain at the end.
- A 75-80°C finishing temperature preserves a fresher milk flavour. Preheated cups reduce heat loss during service.

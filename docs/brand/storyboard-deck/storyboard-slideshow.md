## Slide 01 — Story (Cover) ★ 6-slide web cut

![Slide 01 Cover](images/slide-01-cover.jpg)

**Run a craft bakery without the 4:00 AM burnout.**
Mika bakes brown-butter financiers, savoury beef pies, and ribboned gift boxes in a single Tokyo shop. Seams Pantry connects her recipe bench, Sous Chef agents, and live counter so craft scales without chaos.

---

## Slide 02 — The Four Bench Pressures

![Slide 02 Four Pain Points](images/slide-02-four-pain-points.jpg)

**Four bench pressures quietly cap every independent bakery.**
Owner-makers like Mika and shift leads like Ken fight the same four physical bottlenecks every shift: 04:00 AM starts, hours of sequential prep, batch-to-batch drift, and sink mountains that keep staff past closing.

---

## Slide 03 — No 4 AM ★ 6-slide web cut

![Slide 03 No 4 AM](images/slide-03-no-4am.jpg)

**Replace the 4:00 AM wake-up with tracked afternoon mise-en-place.**
One baker runs the afternoon prep shift (`13:00–16:52`) into tracked cold holds (`48h` shortcrust, black sesame concentrate, ginger tea decoctions; `72h` braised beef fillings at ≤5 °C). Every lot is 22–26 hours old at the end of next morning's shift.

---

## Slide 04 — Direct-to-Pan Parallel Prep

![Slide 04 Direct to Pan](images/slide-04-direct-to-pan.jpg)

**Weigh direct to pan and run hobs, mixer, and chiller in parallel.**
Ingredients weigh straight onto the Ohaus scale into 1 L braising pans (`780 g` filling for 3 pies) across four Vollrath induction hobs, while the Hobart HL200 mixes and the Maruzen oven schedules its `170 °C → 200 °C` changeover.

---

## Slide 05 — 25g Consistency ★ 6-slide web cut

![Slide 05 25g Consistency](images/slide-05-25g-consistency.jpg)

**Lock crisp edges and tender crumb with 180 g butter and 25 g fill guards.**
Every financier batch weighs `180 g` butter raw before browning (`~22 g` water lost, never topped up) and fills Matsunaga shallow 8-cavity trays at `25 g` per cavity across four signature flavours (`Hazelnut`, `Coffee`, `Matcha`, `Black sesame`).

---

## Slide 06 — Washing (`洗い物`) ★ 6-slide web cut

![Slide 06 Washing](images/slide-06-washing.jpg)

**End the sink mountain with fewest-bowl ordering and scheduled wash cycles.**
Fewest-bowl flavour sequencing (`Hazelnut → Coffee → Matcha → Black sesame`) pairs with scheduled Winterhalter UC-L warewasher turnarounds between mixes so pans come out clean before the next batch needs them.

---

## Slide 07 — Sous Chef in Kitchen & Chat

![Slide 07 Sous Chef](images/slide-07-sous-chef.jpg)

**Hands-free Sous Chef agents keep workflow, stock, and margins in sync.**
Through Portal chat, Slack, and local shop voice, Sous Chef runs inside the operator's own permissions, approvals, and durable receipts — synchronizing the recipe canvas, resource timeline, 3D bakery view, and live Costs & margins sheet.

---

## Slide 08 — Live Counter, QR & Gift Orders

![Slide 08 Pantry Counter](images/slide-08-pantry-counter.jpg)

**One live menu across the Register, QR tables, and online gift boxes.**
When the last pie sells, Seams Checkout updates the counter Register, table QR page, and online gift store to `SOLD OUT` in lockstep, reconciling cash, Square Terminal Japan cashless, and gift orders in one ledger.
